# UniGate — Assumptions & Open Questions

**Status:** Phase 1 — living document, updated as answers arrive
**Last updated:** 2026-09-14

The RFP is a two-page commercial document. It defines the *what* at a high level and almost none of the business rules needed to build a working transaction platform. Rather than stall, the design makes explicit, configurable assumptions and records them here.

**Two rules govern this document:**

1. **No assumption is hard-coded.** Every value below is a `system_settings` row, a `commission_rules` row, or an environment variable. Changing an answer is a configuration change, not a code change — unless explicitly marked otherwise.
2. **No assumption is presented to UniGate as a requirement.** Anything below marked `ASSUMED` was chosen by the delivery team and needs confirmation.

Legend — **Impact if wrong:** `LOW` config change · `MEDIUM` code change in one module · `HIGH` schema or cross-module change.

## 3. Explicitly not claimed

The design deliberately makes **no compliance claim** for any of the following. Each requires assessment by a qualified party before UniGate may represent otherwise.

| Regime | Position |
|---|---|
| **PDPL** (Saudi Personal Data Protection Law) | Privacy controls, data classification, retention and access controls are designed with PDPL principles in mind. Residency and lawful-basis questions are unresolved (OQ-12). **Requires legal review.** |
| **ZATCA / Fatoora** e-invoicing | Issuer identity resolved (UniGate, A-49) and the clearance/reporting flow is now **designed** (A-50, [ADR-007](decisions/ADR-007-e-invoicing.md)) rather than merely reserved. A mock provider only; no production integration exists. **Applicability, wave and obligations remain unresolved (OQ-04) and require tax advisory review. No compliance is claimed.** VAT operating model is separately open (OQ-24, [ADR-008](decisions/ADR-008-vat-operating-model.md) **Proposed**), as are self-billing approval (OQ-25), supply characterisation under Art 50 (OQ-26) and zero-rating evidence (OQ-27). **Note: ZATCA does not certify or approve e-invoicing solution providers** — its directory carries an explicit disclaimer to that effect, and regulatory liability stays with the taxpayer whichever vendor is used. Any vendor claim of "ZATCA approved" must be treated as marketing. |
| **TGA** transport licensing | Not modelled beyond the generic document subsystem (OQ-13). Research on 2026-09-14 indicates the scope is **wider than assumed** — a separate brokerage licence for UniGate, a rating threshold owners must meet before they may contract with a broker, and a **mandatory unified electronic contract system** that would sit on the booking-confirmation path. None of this is implemented or verified. **Requires legal review by Saudi counsel.** |
| **PCI-DSS** | No card data ever touches UniGate systems — gateway tokens only, which typically indicates SAQ-A scope. Actual scope must be confirmed with the chosen acquirer (OQ-03). |
| **WCAG 2.2 AA** | An engineering *target* with automated checks in CI. An accessibility audit has not been performed. |
