# Request for Proposal (RFP)
Project Title: Vehicle Hiring & Management Platform (Passenger + Goods Transport)
Company: UniGate
Date: October 2025
## 1. Introduction
UniGate invites proposals from qualified software development firms or professionals for the design and development of a comprehensive Vehicle Hiring and Management Platform. The system aims to streamline vehicle rental and logistics operations, covering both passenger transport (buses, cars, vans, etc.) and goods transport (trucks, trailers, pickups, etc.).
## 2. Objective
To build a digital ecosystem consisting of a web platform and mobile application that enables vehicle owners, drivers, and customers to connect seamlessly for hiring and managing transport services for both passenger and goods transportation. Vehicle owners can register and manage their vehicles, drivers, bookings, bids, income, and maintenance, while customers can request, compare, and book vehicles securely.
## 3. Scope of Work
• Design and development of a web portal and mobile applications (Android & iOS).
• Implementation of vehicle management features, including make, model, driver(s), maintenance, availability, booking status, trip history, and GPS tracking.
• Enable a bidding mechanism for trip requests where vehicle owners can offer quotations.
• Customer module: Create and verify an account (via OTP or mobile number) to request trips, compare bids, make bookings, and track trips in real time.
• Finance management for vehicle income, expenses, and commission tracking.
• Admin dashboard for user management, platform analytics, and reporting.
• Integration of payment gateways (Mada, Visa, MasterCard, STC Pay, Apple Pay).
• Support for multilingual interface (English & Arabic).
• Data privacy controls allowing owners to restrict non-business information.
## 4. System Users and Roles
Admin: Manages all users (vehicle owners, customers, and SPOs – Sales Promotion Officers), vehicles, bookings, payments, and system data.
Vehicle Owner / Driver: Registers vehicles, manages bids, trips, and maintenance.
Customer: Requests and books trips, compares bids, and tracks vehicles in real time.
## 5. Deliverables
• Web-based admin and management portal.
• Mobile applications for customers and vehicle owners/drivers.
• Backend database and API integration.
• Payment gateway setup and testing.
• Source code and technical documentation.
• Deployment support and staff training.
## 6. Proposal Requirements
• Company profile and relevant experience in similar projects.
• Proposed technology stack (frontend, backend, database, etc.).
• Detailed project plan and timeline.
• Cost breakdown by module and milestone.
• Maintenance and support terms.
• Ownership and data policy.
## 7. Evaluation Criteria
• Technical expertise and past project portfolio.
• Proposed methodology and technology stack.
• Project cost and timeline feasibility.
• Post-launch support and maintenance offerings.
